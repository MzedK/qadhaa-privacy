My Qadhaa privacy and support

Dar al-Turath al-Islami provides My Qadhaa. The app keeps a personal record of prayers and fasts you choose to make up.

Your journal

Earlier versions that open directly to a journal keep entries on the device. In those versions, you can move entries by exporting a readable backup. The account features below apply to versions that offer sign-in.

When you sign in, DTI’s account service stores your prayer balances, Ramadan years, daily goal and dated prayer and fasting entries so that you can use the same journal on another device. These records can reveal religious practices. Journal access through the app is restricted to the signed-in account. Google handles identity when you use Google sign-in. The app sends journal entries to DTI’s account service. Cloudflare hosts the My Qadhaa account service and its database. Production and preview accounts have separate databases.

Your device keeps a copy for offline use. Changes sync automatically while Qadhaa is open and connected. A journal opened through the existing on-device journal option stays on that device. Device backups managed by Apple or Google may retain app data according to your phone settings.

Your account

The account service processes your email address, username, and sign-in information. Password sign-in stores a password hash. When you choose Google sign-in, Google handles the sign-in. The account service receives the identity details the provider shares. The website uses a sign-in cookie. The installed Android app keeps a session credential in secure device storage. Google sign-in may supply your profile name and image. Resend processes your email address and the delivery of verification and password reset links on DTI’s behalf. It processes your IP address and browser information for session security and to limit repeated sign-in attempts. Use Delete my account in Journal settings to remove your account and the journal on this device. For help requesting deletion outside the app, email admin@daralturath.co.za.

Optional donations

On Android you choose your amount and frequency in Qadhaa. Payment review opens in your browser. DTI records Qadhaa as the source alongside the donation reference for gifts made through this flow. Qadhaa does not send journal entries to the giving page or receive your payment details.

Reminders

If you enable a daily reminder, your phone schedules a local notification. You can disable reminders in Journal settings or change notification permissions in your phone settings.

Delete your My Qadhaa account

To request deletion without the app, email DTI with an account deletion request from your registered address. DTI may confirm that you control the account. Never include your password or your journal entries.

Delete my account in Journal settings deletes your account, the journal in the account database, and the app’s local copy on that device. Cloudflare’s short-term database recovery history can retain earlier versions for up to seven days on the current Free plan. A previously used offline device may still have cached data. Removing its app data clears that copy. Files you exported with an earlier version remain wherever you saved them.

Support

Contact Dar al-Turath al-Islami at admin@daralturath.co.za. Include the app version and phone model when reporting a problem. You can describe the issue without sharing your journal.
