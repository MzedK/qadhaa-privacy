DTI Qadhaa privacy and support

Dar al-Turath al-Islami provides DTI Qadhaa. The app keeps a personal record of prayers and fasts you choose to make up.

Your journal

Your balances, daily goal, Ramadan years and dated entries are stored on your device. DTI Qadhaa works offline. It has no account system, advertising, tracking, analytics or automatic account sync. DTI does not receive your journal. Device backups managed by Apple or Google may retain app data according to your phone settings.

Reminders

If you enable a daily reminder, your phone schedules a local notification. You can disable reminders in Journal settings or change notification permissions in your phone settings.

Backups and deletion

Save backup creates a readable file containing the journal. You choose its destination. Keep backups somewhere private. Restore backup lets you inspect the entry count before replacing your current journal. To clear the journal, open Journal settings and select Erase journal from this device. Delete exported backups separately wherever you saved them.

Support

Contact Dar al-Turath al-Islami at admin@daralturath.co.za. Include the app version and phone model when reporting a problem. You can describe the issue without sharing your journal.
